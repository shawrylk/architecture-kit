Customers report wrong cart totals when they use a discount code:

- WELCOME10 also reduces books that are already on sale. It must not.
- STAFF25 takes off more than 25% on a book that is on sale.
- Some totals are one cent off.

The rule: a code takes its percent off the price the customer pays (the sale price when a book is on sale), only on the lines the code applies to. Round the discount to the nearest cent, half away from zero.

Fix it, and commit your change when you are done.
